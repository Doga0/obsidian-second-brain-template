# /sb-emerge

1. Read `99 System/Index.md` and the MOCs in scope. Without a scope, use all hubs and knowledge notes.
2. Collect the goal, open questions and decisions of each hub, and the "Where it appears" section of each knowledge note. Dig deeper with `search` where needed.
3. Look for:
   - **Recurring themes**: the same problem under different names in 2+ projects
   - **Hidden connections**: notes not yet linked that could feed each other (confirm with `context` that they aren't linked)
   - **Recurring open questions**: unanswered in more than one hub
   - **Competing directions**: projects pulling on the same resources (time, hardware, focus)
   - **Idea combinations**: concrete, testable new ideas from combining two pieces of work (max 3)
4. Give every finding evidence links and a confidence level (high / medium / speculative), and clearly label speculative ones.
5. Show it in the chat; with `--save` or on approval, save `Personal/70 Analyses/` (work scope: `Work/70 Analyses/`) `<date> - Emerge - <scope>.md`. Suggest `/sb-connect` for links, `/sb-distill` for concepts and `/sb-new idea` for ideas. Then `log emerge "<scope>"`.
